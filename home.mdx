## What you can build

- Maintain Ghana mobile money and bank beneficiaries.
- Request, accept, and fund OTC trades.
- Settle eligible trades into a managed tGHS balance.
- Pay Ghana beneficiaries from your tGHS balance.
- Reconcile treasury activity.
- Receive signed OTC lifecycle webhooks.

## Integration model

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete merchant verification">
    Obtain approved KYB and product access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start in sandbox">
    Create a test key and use `/sandbox/v1`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handle responses">
    Read payloads from `data` and treat non-2xx responses as errors.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add webhooks">
    Verify signatures and process events idempotently.
  </Step>
</Steps>
