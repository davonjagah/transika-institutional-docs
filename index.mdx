## What you can build

- Maintain mobile money and bank beneficiaries.
- Request, accept, and fund OTC trades.
- Settle eligible trades into a managed tGHS balance.
- Pay Ghana beneficiaries from your tGHS balance.
- Reconcile activity from the transaction ledger.
- Receive signed OTC lifecycle events through webhooks.

## Integration model

<Steps>
  <Step title="Complete merchant verification">
    API access requires an approved KYB status and access to the product you want to integrate.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start in sandbox">
    Create a test key and send requests through `/sandbox/v1`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handle the common envelope">
    Read successful payloads from `data` and treat non-2xx responses as errors.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add webhooks and idempotent processing">
    Verify webhook signatures, acknowledge deliveries quickly, and make event handling safe to repeat.
  </Step>
</Steps>
